Practical scenario: tenant rent arrears tracker

You manage a rental unit. Rent is 20,000 KES per month. The tenant has a 12-month lease. You must track the tenant's balance each month.

Payment history (the tenant's behaviour):

Months 1 to 4: pays 20,000.
Months 5 to 7: pays 12,000.
Month 8: pays 0.
Months 9 to 12: pays 25,000.

Monthly steps. Do them in this order:

If the balance is above 0, add a late fee. The fee is 5% of the balance carried from last month.
Add the month's rent to the balance.
Work out the month's payment from the history above.
Subtract the payment from the balance.

Notice rules. Check the balance after the payment:

Balance is 0 or less: print Account clear.
Balance is up to 20,000: print Reminder.
Balance is up to 40,000: print Final notice.
Balance is above 40,000: print Legal action warning.

Your program must:

Use a for loop for months 1 to 12.
Use an if, else if and else chain for the payment history.
Use a second if, else if and else chain for the notice.
Print one line per month in this format:
Month 6 | Fee: 400.00 | Paid: 12000 | Balance: 16400.00 | Reminder
Print Half-year statement: total paid 104000 after months 6 and 12. Use the % operator. The number is the total paid so far.
Track the highest balance and the month when it happened. Print both at the end.
Count the months with Account clear. Print the count at the end.

Rules:

Use no functions and no arrays.
Use const for values that never change. Use named constants for rent, late fee rate and notice limits.
Use ===, not ==.
Use toFixed(2) for the fee and the balance. Money values with decimals need it.
Match the output format exactly.

Before you code:

Predict the balance after month 5. Then predict month 6.
Write the order of the four monthly steps on paper. If you swap steps 1 and 2, the late fee changes.

Checkpoints to test your code:

Month 7 balance is 25220.00.
Month 8 balance is 46481.00. This is the highest balance.
The final balance is 34947.32.

Self-check: Total rent charged is 240,000. Total paid is 216,000. Total late fees are the value you print in your program. Your formula must hold: charged + fees - paid = final balance. Add the total late fees to your output and test it.

Hints:

The payment chain needs range checks with &&. Review the matatu code.
Month 8 has a payment of 0. Test that your balance still grows correctly.
Declare your tracking variables before the loop. Variables declared inside the loop reset each month.
