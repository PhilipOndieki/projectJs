
Practical scenario: rent monthlySaving plan

You are saving for a rent deposit. The goal is 50,000 KES.

In month 1, you save 3,000 KES.
Each month, you save 500 KES more than the month before.

Write a program that:

Uses a while loop. The loop stops when the total saved reaches the goal or goes past it.
Prints one line for each month. Example: Month 1: saved 3000, total 3000.
Prints High saving month on the same month if that month's saving is more than 5,000 KES. Use if.
Prints Quarter check on every third month. Use the % operator.
Prints the number of months needed when the loop ends.

Rules:

Do not use functions or arrays. You have not covered them yet.
Predict the answer before you code. How many months do you expect? Write your guess down.
Take care with the loop. If the total never changes, the loop runs forever.

Check your work:

Month 1 must print a total of 3000.
Check that the loop stops on the correct month. Test the edge case where the total is close to 50,000.
